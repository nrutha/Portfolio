Recognition

Junior Scientist Award

For the Multipurpose Agricultural Robot and Smart-H.

I trained as a mechatronics engineer at SASTRA University, alongside a Foundation in Data Science from IIT Madras. The degree covered industrial robotics, autonomous robots, PLC and automation, control systems and power electronics, and included team projects: an agricultural robot, a building-cleaning robot, a wearable for two-wheeler riders and a 14-DOF manipulator.

In industry I led projects at i6 Technologies and Crobot Technologies, then joined the automation team at Salcomp Industries. There I was responsible, on the team's behalf, for Site Acceptance Testing on two new product introductions, coordinated nine stakeholder groups, and carried out Kaizen with documented savings.

The M.Sc. in Industrial Engineering & Management at Linköping University, in the Innovations domain, adds the management side to that engineering base: innovation management, international business, operations strategy, sustainability transition and entrepreneurship. I apply it in a business analysis and BPR engagement with Polar Light Technologies and in research on the EU Digital Battery Passport.

I am building my career in Europe, with Sweden first, in roles that connect engineering, business strategy, innovation and management.

Not a move away from engineering. An expansion of it.
